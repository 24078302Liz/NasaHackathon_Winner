THE MISSION

Make space fire research easier to explore.

Connect natural-language questions to NASA microgravity combustion research, with cited summaries, relevance ranking, and experiment comparisons.

When configured, this interface calls the FastAPI service for search, investigation records, and comparisons. It displays retrieved passages and marks missing test-level information instead of estimating it. It does not provide fire-safety predictions or operational recommendations.
